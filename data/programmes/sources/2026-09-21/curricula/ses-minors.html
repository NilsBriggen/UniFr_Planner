Minors

The Faculty of management, economics and social sciences offers at Bachelor level, minors worth 60 ECTS and minors worth 30 ECTS open to students from all study paths. At Master level, 30 ECTS minors are also available, with specific admission requirements depending on the programme.

Bachelor‑level minors are offered exclusively in German or French.

Bachelor Minors

BA 30 ECTS

The 30 ECTS minors give the opportunity to acquire foundational knowledge in the chosen field of study.

BA 30 ECTS Gestion d’entreprise, Informatique de gestion, Économie politique et Communication et média (FR)

BA 30 ECTS Betriebswirtschaftslehre, Wirtschaftsinformatik, Volkswirtschaftslehre, Kommunikationswissenschaft und Medienforschung (DE)

BA 30 ECTS Informatique de gestion (pour branche principale informatique) (FR) 

BA 30 ECTS Wirtschaftsinformatik (BA-Hauptfach Informatik) (DE)

Study Plans Full Version

BA 60 ECTS

The 60 ECTS minors offer a broad overview of the chosen field and meet the foundational academic requirements for admission to Master’s‑level study within the same field.

BA 60 ECTS Gestion d’entreprise, Informatique de gestion, Économie politique et Communication et média (FR)

BA 60 ECTS Betriebswirtschaftslehre, Wirtschaftsinformatik, Volkswirtschaftslehre, Kommunikationswissenschaft und Medienforschung (DE)

BA 60 ECTS Informatique de gestion (pour branche principale informatique) (FR)

BA 60 ECTS Wirtschaftsinformatik (BA-Hauptfach Informatik) (DE)

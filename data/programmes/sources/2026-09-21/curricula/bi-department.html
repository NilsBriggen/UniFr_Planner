Minor in Information Systems (Bachelor)

A summary of the key points regarding the minor in business informatics at the bachelor level is given below. More detailed information can be found on the website of the  Faculty of Economics and Social Sciences.

The minor in business informatics is bilingual. Please note that specific rules, outlined towards the bottom of this page, apply for students majoring in computer science.

Further information

  • Minors, Faculty of Economics and Social Science
  • Substantial Minor in Information Systems (60 ECTS)
    Mandatory courses (42 ECTS):
    • Informatique de gestion I / Wirtschaftsinformatik I (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Informatique de gestion II / Wirtschaftsinformatik II (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Information Systems Modeling (6 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Operations Management (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Einführung in die Programmierung (6 ECTS, Language: DE & FR)
    • Introduction à la gestion d'entreprise / Einführung in die Betriebswirtschaftslehre (6 ECTS, Language: DE & FR)
    • Comptabilité / Unternehmensrechnung (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    At least 18 ECTS credits from:
    • Info I: Sécurité informatique (4.5 ECTS, Language: FR)
    • Info II: Decision Support I (4.5 ECTS, Language: DE)
    • Machine Learning (5 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Information Systems Development Project (4.5 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Requirements Engineering for Information Systems (4.5 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Info V: Decision Support II (4.5 ECTS, Language: FR)
    • Algorithmics (6 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Programmation orientée objet (6 ECTS, Language: FR)
    • Génie logiciel: méthodes orientées objets (6 ECTS, Language: FR)
    • GES III: Comptabilité de gestion / BWL III: Controlling (4.5 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • GES IV: Management Stratégique / BWL IV: Strategisches Management (4.5 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Organisation (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • MAN04 Comptabilité financière / Bilanzierung (4,5 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • MAN01 Investissement et financement (6 ECTS, Langue : FR & DE)

    Note: majors taking Computer Science as a supplementary discipline cannot take the "Introduction to Programming" course and must take the "Object Oriented Programming" course instead.

  • Smaller Minor in Information Systems (30 ECTS)
    Mandatory courses (30 ECTS):
    • Informatique de gestion I / Wirtschaftsinformatik I (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Informatique de gestion II / Wirtschaftsinformatik II (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Information Systems Modeling (6 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Operations Management (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Introduction à la gestion d'entreprise / Einführung in die Betriebswirtschaftslehre (6 ECTS, Language: DE & FR)
  • Substantial Minor in Information Systems (60 ECTS) for Computer Science Majors 
    Mandatory Courses (33 ECTS):
    • Informatique de gestion I / Wirtschaftsinformatik I (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Operations Management (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Information Systems Development Project (4.5 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Requirements Engineering for Information Systems (4.5 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Introduction à la gestion d'entreprise / Einführung in die Betriebswirtschaftslehre (6 ECTS, Language: DE & FR)
    • Comptabilité / Unternehmensrechnung (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    At least 27 ECTS credits from:
    • Info I: Sécurité informatique (4.5 ECTS, Language: FR)
    • Info II: Decision Support I (4.5 ECTS, Language: DE)
    • Info V: Decision Support II (4.5 ECTS, Language: FR)
    • Investissement et financement / Investitions- und Finanzierungstheorie (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Marketing (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • GES I: Gestion des ressources humaines / BWL I: Personalökonomie (4.5 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • GES III: Comptabilité de gestion/ BWL III: Controlling (4.5 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • GES IV: Management Stratégique / BWL IV: Strategisches Management (4.5 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Organisation (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • MAN04 Comptabilité financière / Bilanzierung (4,5 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • MAN01 Investissement et financement (6 ECTS, Langue : FR & DE)
  • Smaller Minor in Information Systems (33 ECTS) for Computer Science Majors 
    Mandatory courses (33 ECTS):
    • Informatique de gestion I / Wirtschaftsinformatik I (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Operations Management (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Information Systems Development Project (4.5 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Requirements Engineering for Information Systems (4.5 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Introduction à la gestion d'entreprise / Einführung in die Betriebswirtschaftslehre (6 ECTS, Language: DE & FR)
    • Comptabilité / Unternehmensrechnung (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
  • Smaller Minor in Information Systems (30 ECTS) for students with substantial Minor in Management (30 or 60 ECTS)
    Mandatory courses (30 ECTS):
    • Informatique de gestion I / Wirtschaftsinformatik I (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Informatique de gestion II / Wirtschaftsinformatik II (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Information Systems Modeling (6 ECTS, Language: EN)
    • Operations Management (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
    • Introduction à la programmation / Einführung in die Programmierung (6 ECTS, Language: FR & DE)
